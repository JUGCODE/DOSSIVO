# DOSSIVO

## Supply-chain evidence for EU trade

DOSSIVO helps exporters organize evidence, identify gaps, and share buyer-ready records under company control.

**[Open the live demo in English →](https://dossivo-outcome-workbench.xugv.chatgpt.site/en/)**

### Try the product

The synthetic demo workflow does not require an account.

1. Open **Free self-check** and answer the readiness questions.
2. Run the sample evidence extraction and review the gaps and remediation tasks.
3. Confirm the sample facts and generate Chinese, English and Italian evidence cards.
4. Authorize a limited-view, expiring link and inspect the buyer-facing record. The link can be revoked.

### What this version demonstrates

- A guided workflow from self-check to a reviewable buyer evidence pack.
- Traceable sample facts, visible evidence gaps and human confirmation before disclosure.
- Multilingual views of the same confirmed facts.
- Version-frozen sharing links with expiry, view limits and access records.
- Separate anonymous browser workspaces instead of a shared demo account.

### Current stage and boundaries

This is a working product prototype using **synthetic data**. The sample extraction is deterministic; it is not a live document-processing or externally connected AI service. Real original-file uploads are not enabled. Please do not enter confidential information.

PACIscan registration is not live in this demo. The full evidence-preparation workspace and newsletter preferences require sign-in. Sample professional profiles are fictional. A sharing link is accessible to whoever holds it within its limits; naming a recipient does not authenticate that recipient.

DOSSIVO supports compliance readiness and evidence management. It does not provide legal certification or replace professional legal advice.
